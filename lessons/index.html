Meet the Bluffton Golf Professionals

A professional team focused on clear instruction and steady progress.

Tim Ashley

Director of Golf, PGA Member

Tim has been passionate about golf for more than 30 years and has been a PGA of America Member for more than 20 years. His teaching emphasizes sound fundamentals, consistency, and confidence, helping golfers of every skill level build lasting improvement in both their swing and overall game.

Matthew Ingenito

Assistant Golf Professional, PGA Associate

A PGA Associate, Matthew is committed to growing the game through positive, structured instruction. He works with golfers of every experience level, focusing on skill development, confidence, and clear coaching that makes the learning process both productive and enjoyable.

Joe Elia

Golf Instructor

A lifelong golfer, Joe teaches with a simple, effective philosophy grounded in fundamentals and confidence. He makes improvement approachable and enjoyable, helping players of every skill level develop their games without unnecessarily complicating the learning process.

Gary Guerin

Assistant Golf Professional

Gary brings a professional background in golf instruction and management, with an emphasis on fundamentals and practical game development. His coaching focuses on consistency, short-game improvement, and techniques golfers can carry directly onto the course.

Sean Ritter

Assistant Golf Professional

A Charlotte, North Carolina native, Sean Ritter brings a lifelong passion for golf and a strong foundation in professional instruction and club operations. He graduated at the top of his class from the Professional Golfers Career College in Bluffton with a degree in Professional Golf Management, then spent four years at the Palmetto Dunes Golf Academy working under noted instructor and former tour professional Doug Weaver.

Cydney Rose

Assistant Golf Professional

A retired United States Marine Corps First Sergeant with more than 21 years of service, Cydney Rose brings a strong background in leadership, athletics, and coaching to golf instruction. A graduate of the Professional Golfers Career College, she has coached both golf and volleyball and represented Team USA at the 2023 Invictus Games. Her teaching emphasizes fundamentals, confidence, discipline, and helping golfers of all skill levels reach their full potential.

FAQ

Helpful answers before you request a lesson.

How much do golf lessons cost at Bluffton Golf Club?+

Members pay $80 with the Director of Golf and $50 with the Assistant Golf Professional. Non-members pay $100 with the Director of Golf and $70 with the Assistant Golf Professional.

Do members receive different lesson pricing?+

Yes. Members receive the member pricing listed above for the applicable instructor category.

When are the weekly golf clinics?+

The Ladies Only Clinic is every Tuesday from 4:00 to 5:00 PM, and the Weekly Clinic is every Thursday from 4:00 to 5:00 PM. Clinics are $40 for members and $50 for non-members.

Where is Bluffton Golf Club located?+

Bluffton Golf Club is located at 1 Eagle Pointe Drive, Bluffton, SC 29909.

How do I request a golf lesson?+

Use the lesson request form on this page or open the direct lesson request link above to submit your information.

Who should I call with lesson questions?+

You can call Bluffton Golf Club at (843) 757-5900.
