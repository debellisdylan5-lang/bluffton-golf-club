Public Golf Near Hilton Head: Why Bluffton Belongs on Your Golf Trip

By Bluffton Golf Club September 3, 2026 Visitor Guide

The short answer

Golfers staying on or visiting Hilton Head can also consider public golf in Bluffton. Bluffton Golf Club is a Davis Love III-designed public-golf option in Bluffton, South Carolina, featuring 18 holes, a par-71 layout, and six tee options.

A Hilton Head golf trip does not have to stop at the island’s edge. For visitors researching public golf near Hilton Head, looking at Bluffton can widen the list of courses under consideration and add another Lowcountry setting to the itinerary.

The useful planning question is not whether Bluffton should replace an island round. It is whether a trip can become more varied by including public golf Bluffton, SC visitors can evaluate alongside their other plans. Bluffton Golf Club gives golfers a clearly defined option: a Davis Love III design, 18 holes, par 71, and six tee options.

A broader golf itinerary

Why Bluffton expands a Hilton Head golf trip

Searching for golf near Hilton Head Island often begins with location, but a strong trip plan also considers variety, tee suitability, course identity, and booking logistics. Adding Bluffton to the search creates another category of public-golf possibilities without requiring golfers to treat every round as the same experience.

This is especially relevant for groups building a multi-round schedule. Different golfers may prioritize different tees, yardages, or architectural experiences. A Bluffton golf course can be considered as its own round rather than merely as an extension of an island itinerary.

Bluffton Golf Club’s known details make the initial evaluation straightforward. Golfers can review a Davis Love III-designed, 18-hole, par-71 layout with six tee options, then call BGC to confirm availability and the current information needed for their visit.

Architecture and experience

What the Davis Love III design brings to the conversation

A named designer gives golfers a meaningful point of reference when choosing among public-golf options. At Bluffton Golf Club, the Davis Love III design is part of the course’s identity and a practical reason for architecture-minded players to include BGC in their research.

The most responsible way to approach that fact is without assigning unsupported intentions or quotations to the designer. Instead, visitors can use the design credit as an invitation to pay attention: consider how the course asks for decisions from the tee, how the selected tee changes those decisions, and how the holes unfold across a complete par-71 round.

Who the six tee options help

Six tee options help a course accommodate golfers with different distances, experience levels, and preferences. They can be particularly useful for traveling groups whose players do not all approach the game in the same way.

The best tee is not automatically the longest one a player can attempt. A more useful choice considers typical carry distance, overall consistency, and the kind of challenge the golfer wants that day. Mixed groups can choose different markers while still sharing the round together.

Before play, ask which tee best suits your game and review the course information available at check-in. Thoughtful tee selection can make the course relevant to a wider range of players.

Before you go

Planning a round at Bluffton Golf Club

Start by deciding where the round fits within the larger trip. If you are coordinating several golfers, collect player counts and likely tee preferences before contacting the club. This makes it easier to ask focused questions and confirm arrangements.

Quick answers

Public golf near Hilton Head FAQ

Can visitors to Hilton Head play public golf in Bluffton? +

Yes. Golfers staying on or visiting Hilton Head can consider public golf options in Bluffton, including Bluffton Golf Club.

Who designed Bluffton Golf Club? +

Bluffton Golf Club is a Davis Love III design.

What are the course measurements at Bluffton Golf Club? +

Bluffton Golf Club is an 18-hole par-71 course with six tee options: Champion (Gold) at 6,692 yards, Pro (Blue) at 6,379, Member (White) at 6,005, Hybrid (W/G) at 5,722, Club (Green) at 5,559, and Forward (Red) at 4,897.

Where is Bluffton Golf Club? +

Bluffton Golf Club is located at 1 Eagle Pointe Drive, Bluffton, SC 29909.

How can I ask about booking a tee time? +

Call Bluffton Golf Club at (843) 757-5900 to ask about tee-time availability and current booking details.
