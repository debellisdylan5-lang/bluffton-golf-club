WHY CHOOSE A GOLF OUTING

A golf outing gives your group something a meeting room can’t.

Golf gives clients, coworkers, donors, sponsors, members, or friends several hours together in a relaxed setting with conversation, friendly competition, and a shared experience. It is a straightforward way to bring people together for corporate golf outings, team building, client appreciation, charity golf tournaments, or private golf outings in Bluffton, SC.

WHAT BGC CAN HELP WITH

WE HANDLE THE GOLF. YOU FOCUS ON YOUR GUESTS.

BGC can help coordinate the golf side of the day so the organizer can focus on the group and the purpose of the outing.

Tournament Planning

Work with the organizer on the event format, timing, and overall flow of the golf day.

Player & Cart Setup

Coordinate pairings, cart signs, scorecards, rules, and event-day golf materials as appropriate.

Registration & Arrival

Support a clear registration/check-in setup so groups can get organized before play.

On-Course Contests

Closest-to-the-pin, long-drive, hole-sponsor, and other on-course contest elements can be discussed and coordinated.

Scoring & Awards

Tournament scoring, results, prizes, and the transition into awards after play can be coordinated.

Food & Hospitality

Food and beverage options can be incorporated before or after the round and planned around the group.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of outings can BGC host?

Corporate golf outings, client appreciation events, team-building and employee-recognition outings, charity golf tournaments, networking events, community or association outings, and private group golf days can all be discussed with the BGC team.

How large can a golf outing be?

Outings can accommodate groups of approximately 25–100 players, with final availability and format confirmed during planning.

Can BGC help organize the tournament format?

Yes. Flexible formats and event coordination can be discussed as part of planning.

Can you help with registration, scorecards, contests, scoring, and awards?

Yes. Registration, scorecards, contests, scoring, and awards can be discussed and coordinated as part of the outing planning process.

Are food and beverage options available?

Yes. Food and beverage options can be incorporated into the outing and planned around the group.

How do I start planning?

Call (843) 757-5900 or use the form above and share your group type, approximate size, preferred date or timing, and the purpose of the outing.
