Membership starts with golf worth coming back to.

An 18-hole public Davis Love III design in the Lowcountry.

Bluffton Golf Club brings together an 18-hole public layout, par 71, six tee options, and a Champion (Gold) setup that plays to 6,692 yards. The course is shaped by Lowcountry scenery and the kind of variety that keeps a round interesting from one visit to the next.

Membership FAQ

Questions golfers ask before they inquire.

Is Bluffton Golf Club accepting membership inquiries?

Yes. Use the form below to request current information and speak with the membership team.

What does BGC membership include?

Confirmed membership benefits include discounts, preferred tee times or preferred access, member-only events, and guest privileges. Current terms and details are provided directly by the club.

Who should consider membership at BGC?

Locals, frequent golfers, seasonal residents, or anyone who wants a home course and a deeper connection to the club may want to explore membership.

Can I play BGC if I’m not a member?

Yes, Bluffton Golf Club is a public golf course. Membership is for golfers looking for a deeper relationship with the club.

How do I get current membership pricing?

Pricing is not published on this page. Request current membership information through the form and the club will follow up.
