Davis Love III & the Lowcountry

Where Davis Love III’s Harbour Town legacy meets Bluffton golf.

Five wins at the RBC Heritage—more than any other player—didn’t come from one great week. They came from a career built on preparation, patience, and a refusal to settle for good enough. That same standard shapes Davis Love III’s approach to course design. At BGC, the result is a layout that asks golfers to think, choose the right line, and execute with purpose—rewarding smart decisions, imagination, and committed shots.

  • A record built on excellence. Five RBC Heritage victories at Harbour Town—more than any other player—show Love’s ability to master Lowcountry golf through discipline, preparation, and detail.
  • A champion’s mindset, built into the course. Love designs the way he competed: every angle, landing area, and green asks for a plan—not just power.
  • That philosophy lives at BGC. BGC brings that same standard to Bluffton—a public course that rewards strategy, confidence, and the pursuit of a better round.

FAQ

Fast answers for golfers and guests.

Is Bluffton Golf Club open to the public?

Yes. Bluffton Golf Club is a public golf course in Bluffton, South Carolina.

Who designed the course?

Bluffton Golf Club is a Davis Love III design.

How long is the course?

Bluffton Golf Club plays to 6,692 yards from the Champion (Gold) tees.

Where is the club?

Bluffton Golf Club is located at 1 Eagle Pointe Drive, Bluffton, SC 29909.

How do I book a tee time?

Use the Book a Tee Time button to open Bluffton Golf Club’s live booking page, or call (843) 757-5900 for assistance.

Does the club offer lessons?

Yes. Bluffton Golf Club offers golf instruction in Bluffton, SC. Visit the Lessons page to learn about instructors and submit an inquiry.

Does the club offer memberships?

Yes. Bluffton Golf Club offers golf membership with discounts, preferred tee times, member-only events and guest privileges. Visit the Membership page to learn more and submit an inquiry.
