Bluffton Golf Club

Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

Your privacy matters.

This policy explains the information our website may collect and how Bluffton Golf Club may use it.

Information we may collect

When you use the Bluffton Golf Club website, you may choose to provide information through our Membership, Lessons, Outings, and Contact forms. That information may include your name, email address, phone number, and details about your inquiry or requested service.

The website may also collect basic technical and usage information, such as device type, browser type, pages viewed, referring pages, and general website interaction or analytics data.

How information may be used

Bluffton Golf Club may use collected information to:

  • Respond to questions, requests, and membership inquiries.
  • Provide requested golf, lesson, outing, booking, or club services.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the website.
  • Measure website performance and understand how visitors use the site.
  • Support booking tools and other club-related functionality.

Service providers and third-party services

We may use service providers that support website hosting, online forms, analytics, booking functionality, and related club services. These providers may process information as needed to perform services on our behalf.

The website may also link to or use third-party websites and tools. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy practices. We encourage visitors to review the privacy information provided by any third-party service they choose to use.

Data security

We take reasonable steps intended to protect information associated with the website. However, no online system, transmission method, or storage process can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as our website, services, or information practices change. When an update is made, the effective date shown at the top of this page may be revised.

Privacy questions

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or the website’s information practices, please call Bluffton Golf Club at (843) 757-5900.
