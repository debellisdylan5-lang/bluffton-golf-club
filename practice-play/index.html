Frequently Asked Questions

Practice + Play Details

How long is this membership valid?

Your membership privileges run through December 31, 2026. One upfront fee of $199 covers you for the remainder of the year.

When do the afternoon rates apply?

The discounted $30 for 18-hole and $22 for 9-hole rates become active daily after 2:00 PM. For any rounds booked before 2:00 PM, you receive 10% off the standard rate.

How do I schedule my complimentary golf lesson?

Once your membership is finalized by our pro shop staff, we will coordinate your 1-on-1 session directly with our club teaching professional at your convenience.

What happens once 20 golfers sign up?

This promotion will be promptly closed to preserve course access and high-quality conditions for all members. We encourage you to submit your claim as soon as possible.
